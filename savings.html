Savings Tips
💡
50/30/20 Rule. Allocate 50% of income to needs, 30% to wants, and 20% to savings and debt repayment.
🔄
Automate savings. Set up an automatic transfer on payday so you never miss a contribution to your lockbox.
📈
High-yield savings. Consider a high-yield savings account to earn more interest on your emergency fund and lockbox.
